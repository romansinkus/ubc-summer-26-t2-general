## How to use this

Each round has a **target position** and a **20-deep list in my order of preference**. On the clock, run down the list and take the first name still available. The symbols tell you whether to spend the pick or wait:

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔥 | Probably already gone. Take him instantly if he somehow fell to you. |
| ✓ | **Right on your pick.** This is the guy — take him. |
| ⏳ | Will very likely still be there next round. **Don't spend this pick on him.** |

The ⏳ tags come from an ADP model: 55% FPL's official `draft_rank` (what your leaguemates' board says) + 45% my ranking. It's an estimate, not a guarantee — if two managers go off-script early, everything shifts. Trust the ordering more than the symbols.

`Pts` = 2025/26 final FPL points.

**Three standing rules that override the plan:**

1. **If Gabriel is on the board at your R1 or R2 pick, take him.** He's a defender who scored 209 when DEF #10 scored 137. Then shift one of your later DEF rounds to MID or FWD.
2. **Forwards have a hard cliff at roughly pick 43.** After Calvert-Lewin the position falls off a shelf and stays flat for 60 picks.
