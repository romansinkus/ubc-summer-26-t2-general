## Three things that decide this draft

**1. FPL's own draft board is broken, and most of your league will use it.**
FPL's `draft_rank` is derived from *price tier*, not projected points. In a draft there are no prices, so it systematically buries proven high scorers who happen to be cheap. The gap is enormous:

| Player | FPL draft rank | 2025/26 points |
|---|---|---|
| Tyrick Mitchell (CRY) | 378 | 135 |
| Verbruggen (BHA) | 379 | 130 |
| Petrović (BOU) | 380 | 124 |
| Leno (FUL) | 381 | 122 |
| Kadıoğlu (BHA) | 382 | 118 |
| Cash (AVL) | 383 | 117 |
| Van Hecke (TOT) | 218 | 148 |
| Kelleher (BRE) | 219 | 143 |
| Roefs (SUN) | 220 | 136 |

Kelleher outscored every keeper last season except Raya and is FPL's 219th-ranked player. **This is where your edge is.** Never reach for these guys — just let the board come to you and hoover them up in rounds 8–13.

**2. Forwards run out first.** 8 × 3 = 24 forward slots, and there are maybe 16 nailed-on Premier League starting strikers. After roughly pick 90 the forward pool is genuinely empty — you'll be starting someone's backup. Don't finish round 6 with fewer than two.

**3. Nine new managers.** Maresca replaced Guardiola at City, Iraola replaced Slot at Liverpool, Alonso at Chelsea, Glasner at Forest, Rose at Bournemouth, Sage at Palace, Arbeloa at Fulham, and Jaissle was appointed at Newcastle *today* (5 Aug). Every one of those is a role-certainty risk. When two players are close, take the one whose manager isn't new.

**Opening fixtures (GW1–6):** Man Utd (Hull A, Ipswich H to start), Liverpool, Everton, Newcastle, Man City and Brentford all get soft runs. Bournemouth get the worst. Coventry and Hull are best avoided until the international break.

## Pick-slot game plan

You haven't drawn your slot yet, so:

- **Picks 1–2** — Haaland or Bruno, no thought required. Your next pick is #15/#16, so plan on a defender there: Senesi, Tarkowski or O'Reilly. Don't panic-take a forward.
- **Picks 3–4** — **Take Gabriel.** A defender who scored 209 while DEF #10 scored 137 is the single biggest positional edge available, and Saliba's back injury (no return date) makes Gabriel even more central to Arsenal's set pieces. This is the pick most likely to be misplayed by an opponent following FPL's board.
- **Picks 5–8** — You get back-to-back picks at every turn. Pair a Tier 2 midfielder with a Tier 2 defender at the 1/2 turn, then take your forward pair at the 2/3 turn before that pool thins.

**Round shape, roughly:**

| Rounds | Target |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Two Tier 1/2 assets, at least one non-forward |
| 3–4 | A top-6 defender **and** a starting striker |
| 5–6 | Midfield 2 and 3, prioritising DefCon floor (Rice, Anderson, Enzo, Garner types) |
| 7 | GK1 — but only here, not earlier (see below) |
| 8–11 | Defenders 3–5 from the underrated group; forward 2 |
| 12–13 | Forward 3, GK2 |
| 14–15 | Fliers on minutes and returning players |

**On goalkeepers:** 16 of the 20 starters get drafted, so you can't wait forever — but the drop-off is flat. Raya (162) is a genuine tier of one; after him Kelleher 143, Roefs 136, Pickford 135, Donnarumma 135, Henderson 131 and Verbruggen 130 are separated by 13 points *over a whole season*. Take Raya if he falls to round 5–6, otherwise wait until round 7–8 and take whichever of that cluster is left. Your GK2 can wait until round 13.

## Three caveats

- **The window is open until 31 August — after your draft and after GW1.** Two live moves would change goalkeeper values directly: Trafford leaving Man City for Leeds (which makes him a starter and costs Perri the shirt) and Vicario leaving Spurs (which leaves Kinsky and Dubravka splitting an unpredictable job). Recheck both before you commit a GK2.

- **Set-piece and penalty duties aren't in the official feed yet** — every `penalties_order` field is still null, and the DefCon counters have been reset for the new season. Re-check confirmed pen takers the week of the deadline; that alone can move a player 20+ points.
- **The point totals are last season's.** They're the right backbone because the scoring system is essentially unchanged for 2026/27 (DefCon stays as-is; the bonus-point system was retuned to favour keepers, full-backs and attackers and to reduce overlap with DefCon, and the DefCon formula was recalibrated slightly toward holding midfielders). But they don't know about transfers or the nine new managers — that's what the notes column is for.
