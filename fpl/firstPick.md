### The worst case is better than it looks

FPL's own board ranks **Saka 3rd, Palmer 4th and Isak 5th**. They scored 157, 114 and **41** points last season. None of them is on this list.

Anyone drafting off the default board burns an early pick on one of those three, so the realistic seven names gone before pick 8 are Haaland, Bruno, Saka, Palmer, Isak, Semenyo, Gabriel. That leaves **Igor Thiago, Gibbs-White, Rice *and* João Pedro** still available at the back of round 1.

### Two deliberate changes from the top 8 in draft.md

- **Igor Thiago moves up to 5.** Forwards cliff at roughly pick 43, and there are only ~16 nailed-on starting strikers for 24 roster slots. Banking one early beats the small point gap to Gibbs-White.
- **Elliot Anderson drops off; João Pedro takes the slot.** Anderson is FPL's 31st-ranked player and will almost certainly still be there at your round 2 pick — no reason to spend a guaranteed-value slot on someone you can have anyway. João Pedro at FPL 14 won't reliably last.

### Why Gabriel at every slot from 3 to 7

He scored 209 as a defender when DEF #10 scored 137 — about a 101-point edge over replacement, against ~89 for Semenyo, the best midfielder likely available. Midfield is deep enough that Rice, Anderson, Rogers or Szoboszlai will still be there at your R2 pick; the defender pool falls off a cliff after about six names. **Saliba is out indefinitely**, which makes Gabriel even more central to Arsenal's set pieces.

He's FPL's 7th-ranked player, so there's a real chance he survives to pick 6 or 7 — but don't gamble on it. Take him.

### Slots 1 and 2

The Haaland/Bruno coin flip genuinely doesn't matter: 239 vs 235 points, both the clear best asset at their position. Take whichever you'd rather captain. Expect a top-six defender (Guéhi, Van Dijk, Senesi, Tarkowski) to be your pick at 15 or 16.

### Slot 8 — the best seat in the draft

You pick 8 and 9 back-to-back, so treat it as one decision and take the two scarcest assets. Igor Thiago is the last forward with a genuine ceiling — 181 points, 3,282 minutes, on penalties. Pair him with Guéhi or Van Dijk at 9.

**Do not take two midfielders here.** That's the mistake the pairing invites, and midfield is the one position you can rebuild from in rounds 5–12.
